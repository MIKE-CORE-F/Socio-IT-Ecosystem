# Socio-IT Ecosystem  
### Public Conceptual Layer of the MIKE‑CORE Ecosystemic Architecture

The **Socio‑IT Ecosystem** is the public-facing conceptual layer of the broader  
**MIKE‑CORE ecosystemic IT architecture**, a multi‑layered framework designed to  
help organisations understand, stabilise and evolve complex digital environments  
through ecosystem thinking, physiological modelling and structured governance.

This repository contains the open conceptual models **TRIAD**, **HEXAD** and  
**DODECAD**, which together form the socio‑technical backbone of the MIKE‑CORE  
architecture.

---

## 🌐 Vision

Modern IT environments behave less like machines and more like **ecosystems**.  
They grow, drift, adapt, degrade, regenerate and interact with external forces.  
The Socio‑IT Ecosystem provides a structured way to understand these behaviours  
and to design systems that are:

- **Stable**  
- **Observable**  
- **Governable**  
- **Resilient**  
- **Evolvable**  
- **Ethically aligned**

This layer bridges **human factors**, **organisational dynamics** and  
**technological physiology**, enabling organisations to operate digital systems  
as living, adaptive ecosystems rather than isolated technical components.

---

## 🧩 Core Models

### 1. TRIAD — The Stability Engine  
The TRIAD model defines the three foundational forces that determine ecosystem  
behaviour:

- **Governance** — rules, boundaries, ethics, authority  
- **Validation** — correctness, integrity, truthfulness  
- **Observability** — awareness, feedback, transparency  

Together, these form the **ecosystem stability engine**, ensuring systems remain  
predictable, safe and aligned with organisational intent.

---

### 2. HEXAD — The Constitutional Layer  
The HEXAD model expands the ecosystem into six constitutional domains:

1. **Governance & Policy**  
2. **Continuity & Lifecycle**  
3. **Innovation & Adaptation**  
4. **Risk & Drift Awareness**  
5. **Human & Organisational Factors**  
6. **Technical Physiology & Architecture**

These domains allow organisations to map their digital environment as a  
multi‑dimensional socio‑technical organism.

---

### 3. DODECAD — The 12‑Layer Expansion  
The DODECAD model provides a full 12‑layer ecosystemic expansion, enabling  
fine‑grained analysis, modelling and intervention across:

- Structural layers  
- Behavioural layers  
- Interaction layers  
- Drift layers  
- Isolation zones  
- Controlled work systems  
- AI integration zones  
- Ecosystem boundaries

This model is used to design **ecosystem‑compatible architectures**, including  
AI‑safe environments, reproducible pipelines and governance‑aligned innovation  
zones.

---

## 🧠 Ecosystemic Principles

The Socio‑IT Ecosystem is built on several core principles:

- **Digital systems behave like living ecosystems**  
- **Stability emerges from governance, validation and observability**  
- **Drift is natural but must be monitored and controlled**  
- **Human factors are not external — they are part of the system**  
- **AI must operate inside controlled, governed ecosystem zones**  
- **Architecture must reflect physiology, not just structure**

These principles guide organisations toward sustainable, ethical and resilient  
digital environments.
