HEXAD Model (Public Layer)

This page describes the public HEXAD model of the socio-IT ecosystem designed by Mike Verhelst. Only the external constitutional domains are shown. Internal mappings, core engines and proprietary logic remain private.

Domain 1

Foundation

Defines the basic ecosystem context, purpose and boundary conditions for digital environments.

Domain 2

Structure

Describes how components, layers and relationships are organised in the ecosystem.

Domain 3

Integration

Covers public integration flows, interfaces and interaction channels between ecosystem elements.

Domain 4

Validation

Defines how inputs, processes and outputs are checked against public criteria and constraints.

Domain 5

Operationalisation

Describes how ecosystem behaviour is executed, maintained and adjusted in practice.

Domain 6

Stability

Focuses on resilience, continuity and the ability to absorb change without losing identity.

Author: Mike Verhelst
