DODECAD Model (Public Layer)

This page describes the public DODECAD model of the socio-IT ecosystem designed by Mike Verhelst. Only the external twelve-layer structure is shown. Internal matrices, core engines and proprietary architecture remain private.

Layer 1

Infrastructure

Physical and virtual foundations on which the ecosystem runs.

Layer 2

Connectivity

Networks, links and channels that enable communication and data flow.

Layer 3

Identity & Access

Public concepts of identity, roles and access control.

Layer 4

Data Structures

How information is organised, classified and made usable.

Layer 5

Applications & Services

Functional components that deliver value to users and systems.

Layer 6

Integration Workflows

Public flows that connect applications, data and processes.

Layer 7

Observability & Telemetry

Signals, metrics and logs that describe ecosystem behaviour.

Layer 8

Security & Compliance

Protection, rules and constraints applied to the ecosystem.

Layer 9

Governance & Decision-Making

How decisions are made, risks are managed and continuity is ensured.

Layer 10

Lifecycle & Release Management

Evolution, deployment and controlled change of ecosystem components.

Layer 11

Analysis & Signal Detection

Interpreting signals, patterns and behaviours in the ecosystem.

Layer 12

Innovation & Experimentation

Safe spaces for trying new ideas without destabilising the core.

Author: Mike Verhelst

Only public layers are shown. Internal DODECAD matrices and core architecture remain private.
